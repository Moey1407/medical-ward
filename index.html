How a shift works

  1. Go to your patient. Click the patient with the red !, or press See patient. Your doctor walks there and the encounter opens. No keyboard moving needed.
  2. If they look unwell, hit Orders · treat now straight away. You don't have to finish the history first.
  3. Write or say everything: what matters in their profile, your questions, your exam, your reasoning. The investigations list is the only thing you tick.
  4. Results arrive while you carry on — 1 minute of hospital time = 1 second of real time.
  5. The big Continue button at the bottom always takes you to the next step.
  6. Every patient earns XP. Wrong drugs have consequences — and you'll be told exactly why.
